**ONE-SHOT: NINE MINUTES TO THE TOAST**

Duke Ansel Vairemont is hosting an engagement dinner for his daughter. You have been hired as discreet additional security, which is the polite way of saying you will be seated at the far end of the table and expected to look pleasant. The food is excellent. The guest list is eleven. Nothing about this job should take more than an evening.

Four hours, three of you. Heavy on puzzles and talking. There are fights, but they are not the point.

Tone is comedy. Build someone who would be funny at a dinner party.

**CHARACTER CREATION**

**Use Pathbuilder 2e.** Please build in it even if you normally do sheets by hand. It makes tracking enormously easier on my end. Send me the export link once you are done.

**Level 12.** Any published ancestry, class, or archetype. Remaster preferred, legacy fine, just tell me which.

**Attributes:** standard boost method, cap of 18 at level 1, plus your boosts at 5 and 10.

**Feats you should have:** ancestry at 1, 5, 9. Class at 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. Skill at 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. General at 3, 7, 11. Skill increases at 3, 5, 7, 9, 11. Master is your ceiling on skills at this level.

**Gear:** one 11th-level item, two 10th, one 9th, two 8th, plus 700 gp. If itemizing sounds like a chore, take 4,500 gp flat and shop, nothing above 11th level.

**Free archetype:** off.

**TWO ASKS**

Talk to each other before you build. Three people means three gaps, and I will not quietly patch it if nobody can heal.

Bring skills, including strange ones. This session pays out on Thievery and Society and Diplomacy and whatever Lore you have been sitting on far more than it pays out on a third damage option. If you own a skill feat you have never once used, this is its session.

You need to know nothing about the setting going in. Everything arrives at the table.

Post builds whenever. No rush, and no obligation to have it done early. =)
